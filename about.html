Our Story

About Siaram

The Name

Marais, Reversed

Derived from Marais — the owner's horse's name, reversed — Siaram represents a personal reflection: strength, freedom, and elegance mirrored back at you. It carries a quiet power, much like these pieces: rooted in nature, motion, and emotion.

About Us

Timeless, intentional, personal

Siaram Jewelry is a fine jewelry brand dedicated to creating timeless pieces that embody elegance, intention, and individuality — designed to feel personal, intentional.

Rooted in the belief that jewelry should be both meaningful and wearable, Siaram focuses on refined, minimalist designs that transition effortlessly from everyday moments to special occasions. The brand also offers bespoke and custom creations, allowing each piece to reflect the personal identity and story of the wearer.

At Siaram, jewelry is more than an accessory — it is a reflection of confidence, strength, and quiet elegance.

Brand Vision

Minimalism meets meaning

To redefine luxury through soulfulness and intentional design — to create jewelry that celebrates the beauty of simplicity, pieces that whisper elegance rather than shout opulence.

Siaram Jewelry envisions a world where minimalism meets meaning: timeless designs that move effortlessly from day to night, carrying stories of confidence, grace, strength, empowerment, and inner light.

"To empower elegance through timeless, everyday jewelry that reflects quiet confidence and personal strength."

Our jewelry isn't made to complete a look — it's made to reflect who you are.

Brand Story

Born from what moves us

Born from the idea that less is more, Siaram Jewelry is an ode to effortless beauty — created for women who carry elegance not in excess, but in essence.

Siaram Jewelry began from a personal love of fine jewelry and the elements that move us — light, nature, motion, and balance. Drawn to delicate pieces you could wear every day, yet pieces that still feel expressive.

Celestial

A fascination with the moon, sun, and stars — symbols of light and guidance.

Equestrian

Freedom, strength, and loyalty, inspired by the bond between a horse and its owner.

Arrow & Geometric

Direction, progress, and ambition — representing resilience, protection, and moving forward.

Craftsmanship

Made with care, made to last

Each design starts with a simple sketch and goes through several rounds of refinement before it becomes a finished piece. We work in gold and hand-select every stone, favoring quality and comfort over trend.

Because these are pieces meant for daily wear, durability matters as much as design — every clasp, chain, and setting is checked before it reaches you.

Designed to Layer

Every new piece is designed to sit naturally alongside what you already own.

Small Batches

We release in small collections rather than mass runs, so pieces stay considered, not disposable.

Made with Care

From sketch to finished piece, every step is done with attention to detail and comfort.
