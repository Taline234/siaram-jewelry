1. What website storage does

Cookies are small files placed on your device by a website. Similar storage, including local storage, may also remember information between visits. This policy uses "cookies" to include these technologies where relevant. Some are needed for requested website functions; others support optional preferences, analytics or advertising.

2. Necessary and optional uses

Necessary storage supports functions such as security, sign-in, a requested shopping cart and remembering your privacy choice. Optional storage may remember extra preferences, measure visits or support advertising, where those features are enabled. A service's actual purpose determines its category; calling it "necessary" does not make every use necessary.

We use the necessary storage listed below. We do not currently enable optional analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and the available choices before enabling the new use.

We do not set any cookies. If you never add to the cart or sign in, nothing is stored.

No payment or embedded services on our website store anything on your device. Orders are paid in cash or by Whish payment request, outside our website. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and our sign-in library from jsDelivr; these set no cookies.

4. Managing your choices

You can use your browser controls to remove or block stored data. Blocking necessary storage may prevent sign-in, checkout or other requested functions from working. Deleting browser data may remove your saved choices and cause the website to ask again.

For questions about website storage, contact customerservice@siaramjewelry.com. Our Privacy policy explains the other information we handle and how to make a privacy request.
