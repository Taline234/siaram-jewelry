Custom Pieces

Any Siaram piece can be made just for you: a name in Arabic or English, an initial, a birthstone, a stone you already own, or an entirely new design drawn from your idea.

  1. 01

    Share Your Idea

    Send us a sketch, a reference photo, a stone you own, or simply the feeling you want the piece to carry.

  2. 02

    Design Together

    Our goldsmiths propose a drawing, metal, stones and finish, refined with you until every detail is right, down to the spelling. There is no design fee.

  3. 03

    Made for You

    Once you approve the design and pay a 50% deposit, your piece is handmade in 18k gold and ready in three to four weeks.
