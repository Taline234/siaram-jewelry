Made for You

Custom Pieces

Any Siaram piece can be made just for you: a name in Arabic or English, an initial, a birthstone, a stone you already own, or an entirely new design drawn from your idea.

  1. I

    Share Your Idea

    Send us a sketch, a reference photo, a stone you own, or simply the feeling you want the piece to carry.

  2. II

    Design & Quote

    We draw your piece and send you the final design with its price. We refine it with you until every detail is right. There is no design fee.

  3. III

    Made for You

    Approve the design and pay a 50% deposit by Whish within 48 hours. We'll email you once your piece is in production. It's ready in three to four weeks; the other 50% is paid in cash on delivery.After 48 hours, the quote may change with the price of gold.
