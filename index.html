Our Story

Made to be lived in

Siaram began with a simple idea: jewelry should feel like an extension of you, not an occasion piece reserved for special days. Each design is meant to be layered, stacked, and worn every day until it becomes part of your story.

Every piece is made in 18k gold and set with hand-selected stones — delicate enough to wear together, timeless enough to wear alone.
