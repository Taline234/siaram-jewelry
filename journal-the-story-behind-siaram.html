Our Story

The Story Behind Siaram

Siaram Jewelry

Every brand begins with a name. Ours began with a horse.

Marais, reversed

Siaram is Marais — the name of our founder's horse — written backwards. It's a personal reflection: strength, freedom and elegance, mirrored back at you. The name carries a quiet power, and we wanted the jewelry to carry it too.

Less, but meaningful

Siaram began from a love of fine jewelry and the things that move us — light, nature, motion and balance. We were drawn to delicate pieces you could wear every day that still felt expressive. Pieces that whisper elegance rather than shout it.

The ideas we return to

  • Celestial: the moon, sun and stars as symbols of light and guidance — you'll see it in the Crescent Moon Ring.
  • Equestrian: freedom, strength and loyalty, inspired by the bond between a horse and its owner — carried by the Horseshoe Ring and Horseshoe Chain Earrings.
  • Arrow & geometric: direction, progress and ambition — resilience, protection and moving forward.
  • Nature & motion: from freshwater pearls to the whimsical Seahorse Pearl Bracelet.

Made to reflect you

Our jewelry isn't made to complete a look — it's made to reflect who you are. That's why we also create bespoke and personalised pieces, so each one can carry its wearer's own story.
