Every Siaram piece is made in 18K gold. But what does that number actually mean, and how should it guide your choice?

## What 18K means
18K gold is made of 75% gold by weight, blended with other metals that influence its colour and how it can be worked. Pure gold is described as 24K, while 14K contains about 58.3% gold. The number alone does not tell you how a finished piece will wear: design, thickness, setting and craftsmanship also matter.

> 18K gold is 75% pure gold: rich in colour, and strong enough to wear every day.

## The three colours
Yellow gold keeps a warm, traditional appearance. White gold gets its pale tone from the alloy and is often finished with rhodium plating, which may need refreshing over time. Rose gold contains copper-rich alloys that create its pink warmth. All three can be 18K gold.

![Yellow, rose and white gold compared](images/journal/gold-colours-guide.jpg)

## What to consider
Choose the colour you enjoy wearing, ask about alloy sensitivities if relevant, and think about your routine. Fine jewelry is meant to be enjoyed, but even 18K gold can scratch or bend with wear. Treat it as a precious material, not an indestructible one.
