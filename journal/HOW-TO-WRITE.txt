How to publish a journal article
================================

1. Put the photos in images/journal/ (a landscape cover photo looks best).
2. Add a new file in this folder, for example journal/qamar-more-than-a-moon.md, starting like this:

---
title: Qamar: More Than a Moon
date: 2027-02-04
image: images/journal/qamar-cover.jpg
excerpt: One or two sentences that appear under the title, on the Journal page and in the email.
category: Our Stories
pieces: qamar-ring, crescent-moon-ring, star-moon-necklace
---

Only title, date, image and excerpt are required.
- category: one of Our Stories, Gifting, Gold & Gemstones, Custom & Craft, Styling, Care, Buying Guide
  (it is the label on the article and the filter buttons on the Journal page).
- pieces: up to 4 pieces shown as cards under the article and in the email, written as in their web
  address (siaramjewelry.com/product-qamar-ring -> qamar-ring).
- email: leave it out and the article is NOT emailed. "email: yes" emails it when it is published;
  "email: 2027-02-09 10:00" schedules the email for that date and time (Beirut time).
  subject: an email subject line, if different from the title.

Then write the article. Leave an empty line between paragraphs. The first paragraph gets a large first letter.

## A heading                       (### for a smaller one)
- a list item                      (or 1. 2. 3. for a numbered list)
**bold**, *italic*, [a link](shop.html#celestial)
> A sentence worth remembering     (a large quote between gold lines)
![A photo description](images/journal/photo.jpg "An optional caption")
![Second photo](images/journal/photo2.jpg)     (2 or 3 photo lines together = side by side)
[[pieces: sun-ring, sun-necklace]]  (piece cards in the middle of the text)
## Explore further                 (everything after it becomes a green panel at the end)

3. When it is merged to main, the website shows it within a few minutes. Emails go out only for articles
   with an "email:" line, and only once the site has launched.
