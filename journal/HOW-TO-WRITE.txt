How to publish a journal article
================================

1. Put the cover photo in images/journal/ (a landscape photo looks best in the email).
2. Add a new file in this folder, for example journal/the-story-behind-siaram.md, starting like this:

---
title: The Story Behind Siaram
date: 2026-11-12
image: images/journal/story-behind-siaram.jpg
excerpt: One or two sentences that appear on the Journal page and in the email.
---

Then write the article. Leave an empty line between paragraphs.

## A heading
- a list item
**bold**, *italic*, [a link](https://siaramjewelry.com/shop.html)
![A photo description](images/journal/photo.jpg)

3. When it is merged to main, the website shows it within a few minutes and, once the site has
   launched, every subscriber automatically gets it by email (the look of the email is
   tools/email/journal-template.html).
