Every custom piece begins with a feeling or an idea. You might bring a sketch, a reference photograph, a name, a symbolic motif or simply a story you want to wear.

> You can ask for help developing a concept, even without a finished design.

## How it works
The process moves through a few steps: a design brief, a review of what is possible, a quote, your approval of the design, production and final checks. Changes may become limited or chargeable once production begins.

![A pencil sketch of a horse bit bracelet](images/journal/sketch-horse-bit-bracelet.jpg "An idea on paper")
![A pencil sketch of a chain ring](images/journal/sketch-chain-ring.jpg "Exploring the shape")

## Where to start
Contact Siaram with your idea, the gold colour you would like, an approximate budget and your deadline. Custom pieces usually take two to three weeks once the design is approved, so start early for a special date.
