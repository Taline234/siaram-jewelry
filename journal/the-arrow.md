An arrow must be pulled backward before it can move forward. That simple image became the inspiration for this collection. We all encounter delays, uncertainty and moments when progress seems to have stopped. I wanted a symbol that reminds us those moments do not define us.

> An arrow must be pulled backward before it can move forward.

## What the arrow means to me
For me, the Arrow represents direction, focus and strength. It is a reminder that setbacks can become preparation for something greater, and that each of us is moving on our own timeline. It does not promise that every difficult moment has an easy answer. It offers a little encouragement to continue.

## A meaningful gift
The Arrow makes a thoughtful gift for someone starting a new job, finishing studies, moving countries or finding their feet after a difficult year. It says: I see how far you have come, and I believe in what comes next.
