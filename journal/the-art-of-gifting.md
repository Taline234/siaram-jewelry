The best gifts begin with the person, not the occasion. Before you choose, take a moment to think about who they are, what they wear, and what you want the piece to say.

> Start with the person, not the occasion.

## Notice what she already wears
Does she reach for delicate chains, bold earrings or stacked rings? Does she wear yellow gold, white gold or a mix? Look at what she wears most often, not only what she saves for special occasions. If you are unsure about sizing, a necklace or earrings may be easier than a ring.

## Make the moment yours
A birthday can call for a signature everyday piece; a graduation for an Arrow; an anniversary for a custom engraving; a wedding for understated earrings that will still be worn afterward. Add a note explaining why you chose the symbol. That story is often what makes the gift memorable.

## When you cannot decide
If you do not know their style, size or preferred gold, a gift voucher gives them the pleasure of choosing. It is not an impersonal fallback when presented thoughtfully: include a note saying you wanted them to select a piece that feels completely theirs.
