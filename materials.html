Gold Colours

Every Siaram piece is made in solid 18k gold. Each design can be made in yellow, rose or white gold; yellow is usually in stock, and rose and white are made to order.

18k Yellow Gold

The classic warm gold of most Siaram pieces. 18k means 75% pure gold, mixed with silver and copper for strength.

Look: Warm, rich and timeless; flatters warmer and olive skin tones and layers with everything.

Care: Polish gently with a soft cloth. It keeps its colour for life.

18k Rose Gold

75% gold with a little more copper, which gives its soft pink blush.

Look: Romantic and modern; beautiful on most skin tones and with pearls and pink stones.

Care: Copper makes it slightly harder than yellow gold, and the colour deepens gently over the years.

18k White Gold

75% gold mixed with white metals, then finished with a thin layer of rhodium for a bright, silvery shine.

Look: Cool and crisp; makes diamonds and white stones look even brighter.

Care: The rhodium finish can soften with wear; it can be renewed (re-plated) by a jeweller every year or two.

Cuts

The cut is the shape a stone is given. It changes how the stone catches the light and how the piece looks on you.

Round brilliant

The most popular cut, made for maximum sparkle.

Oval

A soft, elongated round that flatters the finger.

Princess

A square cut with brilliant sparkle and clean corners.

Cushion

A square with soft rounded corners; romantic and vintage.

Emerald cut

A long rectangle with cut corners and calm, mirror-like steps.

Pear

A teardrop, round at one end and pointed at the other.

Marquise

A long boat shape with two points; it makes fingers look longer.

Heart

A romantic heart outline.

Baguette

A slim rectangle, often set beside a centre stone or in rows.

Cabochon

Polished smooth and domed, without facets; used for turquoise, opal and onyx.

Settings

The setting is how the gold holds the stone. It changes how much light reaches the stone, how protected it is and the overall style of the piece.

Prong (claw)

Small metal claws hold the stone and let in the most light; classic for solitaires.

Bezel

A thin rim of gold wraps around the stone; smooth, modern and very secure for everyday wear.

Pavé

Many tiny stones set close together, so the surface looks paved with sparkle.

Channel

Stones sit in a row between two walls of gold, with no metal between them; sleek and snag-free.

Halo

A circle of small stones around a centre stone; it makes the centre look larger and brighter.

Flush

The stone sits level with the gold, almost hidden in the metal; minimal and very protected.

Bar

Stones in a row with a thin bar of gold between each one; open and airy.

Cluster

Several stones grouped together to form one larger shape, often a flower.
