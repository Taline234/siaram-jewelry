Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Siaram Jewelry ("we", "us") collects when you visit siaramjewelry.com, create an account, place an order, or contact us — and how we use, share, and protect it.

Siaram Jewelry is based in Lebanon. If you have any question about this policy, contact us using the details in Contact below.

1. Information We Collect

Accounts

If you create an account: your first and last name, email address, phone number, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or older), and password. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form — we cannot see it. We also keep your saved delivery addresses, your wishlist, and when you last used your account.

Orders

When you order, with or without an account: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and any delivery notes, the pieces you ordered and their prices, any gift voucher used, your confirmation that you are 18 or older, and the order's payment and delivery status.

Payments

You pay through Whish in the Whish app. We do not collect or store card numbers, security codes or Whish login details; we only see the payment you send us and its note (your order reference).

Gift Vouchers

The names of the purchaser and recipient, the voucher amount and balance, and where it was used.

Messages

Anything you choose to send us by email, WhatsApp or Instagram.

Technical Information

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic request information (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) in server logs used for security and troubleshooting. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, prepare and deliver your orders, and to confirm your payment.
  • To run your account: signing you in, saving your details, addresses and wishlist, and showing your orders.
  • To send service emails: order confirmations, password resets, and inactive-account notices.
  • To check that customers are 18 or older, and to apply and track gift vouchers.
  • To answer your questions and handle exchanges, repairs and warranty requests.
  • To keep records required for accounting and legal purposes, and to protect our website against misuse.

We do not send marketing emails. If we introduce a newsletter, we will only send it to people who have asked to receive it.

3. Who We Share It With

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the shop, only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Netlify — hosts this website and runs our order and account server code. Data: website requests (including IP address and browser details in server logs) and the order details passing through our server code. Location: United States. Privacy policy.
  • Supabase — our database and sign-in service, which stores accounts, addresses, wishlists, orders and gift vouchers. Data: everything listed under Accounts and Orders above; passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form. Location: European Union (Germany). Privacy policy.
  • Resend — sends our order confirmations and account emails. Data: your name, email address and the content of the email (such as your order summary). Location: United States. Privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Outlook — our business mailbox, where order notifications and your emails to us are received. Data: order notifications and any messages you send us. Location: Microsoft data centres. Privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — provides the typefaces used on this website. Data: your IP address and browser details when the fonts load (no cookies are set). Location: United States. Privacy policy.
  • Whish Money — the payment service you use to pay for your order. Data: payment details are handled by Whish in its own app; we only see the payment you send us and its note. Location: Lebanon. Privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta) — if you choose to contact us there. Data: your messages and profile details on those services. Location: Meta's data centres. Privacy policy.
  • Our delivery courier — delivers your order. Data: your name, phone number and delivery address. Location: Lebanon.

Some of these providers process data outside Lebanon, as listed above. We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

4. Cookies & Browser Storage

This website does not use cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no optional cookies to accept or decline.

To make the shop work, the website stores a few items in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are all strictly necessary and are not shared with anyone:

  • siaram-cart — siaramjewelry.com (first party). Remembers the pieces in your cart. Kept until you remove the items, place your order, or clear your browser data.
  • sb-…-auth-token — siaramjewelry.com (first party, created by our sign-in service, Supabase). Keeps you signed in to your account. Only created when you sign in; removed when you sign out.
  • siaram-last-active — siaramjewelry.com (first party). Notes when your account activity was last recorded, so we know your account is in use (see How Long We Keep It). Only created when you are signed in; kept until you clear your browser data.

No payment or third-party service is embedded in our pages: payments happen in the Whish app. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but does not set cookies.

5. How Long We Keep It

  • Accounts: kept while you use them. After 12 months without activity we email you a reminder; after 24 months we email you that your account will be deleted in 30 days; if you still do not sign in, we delete your account, saved addresses and wishlist. You can also delete your account yourself at any time from My Account.
  • Orders and gift vouchers: kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax, warranty and legal purposes, even if your account is deleted.
  • Messages: kept for as long as needed to help you, and then deleted.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

6. Age Requirement

Our shop is for people aged 18 or older. Accounts cannot be created for anyone under 18, and guests must confirm they are 18 or older at checkout. We do not knowingly collect information about children; if you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it — you can also edit your name, phone and addresses yourself in My Account.
  • Delete it — you can delete your account yourself at any time; we keep order records only as described above.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw any consent you have given.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before responding. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. These rights apply as provided by Lebanese law, including Law No. 81 of 2018 on Electronic Transactions and Personal Data.

8. Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only in hashed form, and database access rules ensure each customer can see only their own account, addresses, wishlist and orders. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as card details to us by email or message.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. We will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will let account holders know by email.

For details on orders, payments, delivery and returns, see our Terms & Conditions.
