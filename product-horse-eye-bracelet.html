The story behind the Regard Bracelet

Regard means “gaze” in French, honouring the quiet trust and connection shared through a horse’s eyes.

Part of our Equestrian Collection. Inspired by the bond between a horse and its rider, and the quiet strength, elegance and timeless spirit of the equestrian world.
