Size Guide

Rings, bracelets, necklaces and anklets: how to measure, and the sizes we make.

RingsBraceletsNecklacesAnklets

Rings

Take a ring that fits the right finger well and measure its inside diameter: straight across the centre, at the widest part, then find it in the first column below. No ring to measure? Follow the four steps below instead.

Get your ring size perfectly at home

  1. Wrap a strip of paper or a piece of thread around the base of your finger.
  2. Mark the point where the ends meet with a pen.
  3. Lay it flat along a ruler and measure up to the mark in millimetres.
  4. Find that circumference in the table below: that is your size.

Bracelets

Necklaces

Each necklace lists its chain length in its details. This chart shows the usual names for each length.

StyleInchesCentimetres
Collar12–13″30–33 cm

Opera28–34″70–86 cm
Ropeover 45″over 115 cm
  • Your neck. Wrap a soft tape measure around the base of your neck and add about 5 cm. That is the shortest chain that will sit comfortably.
  • Crew necks and high necklines. Choose a chain that sits above the neckline (35–40 cm) or well below it (50 cm or more).
  • V-necks and open shirts. A pendant or Y-shaped necklace at 45–50 cm follows the line of the V.
  • Scoop, square and strapless necklines. A choker (35–40 cm) or princess length (43–45 cm) frames the collarbone.
  • Layering. Leave about 5 cm between chains, for example 40, 45 and 50 cm.

Anklets
