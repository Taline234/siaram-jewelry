4. Production & Shipping

Orders generally require up to one week of processing before shipping.

In-Stock Items

Please allow 3–4 business days for your order to be prepared and dispatched. Once your order has been dispatched, you will be notified accordingly.

Delivery times are estimates and may vary slightly during busy periods, holidays, or due to circumstances beyond our control.

Out-of-Stock Items

As each Siaram Jewelry piece is carefully crafted, some designs may occasionally be unavailable. If your selected item is out of stock, please allow a production time of 1–3 weeks before dispatch. We appreciate your patience, as this allows us to ensure every piece meets our quality standards.

If your order contains both in-stock and made-to-order items, it may be shipped once the entire order is ready, unless otherwise agreed.

Shipping & Customs

Shipping timelines and costs (paid by the customer) vary by destination and carrier. Siaram Jewelry is not responsible for postal or courier delays, loss, or damage once an order has been dispatched.

International orders may be subject to customs duties or import taxes determined by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the customer.

5. Returns & Exchanges

At Siaram, every piece is carefully inspected and quality-checked before shipment to ensure it meets our standards.

Returns are only accepted for exchanges. We have a 14-day exchange policy, which means you have 14 days after receiving your item to request an exchange. We do not offer or accept returns for refunds, returns on sale items, or gift cards.

Eligibility

To be eligible for a return for exchange, your item must be:

  • In the same condition you received it.
  • Unworn, undamaged, and unused.
  • In its original packaging.
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase.
  • The subject of an exchange request submitted within 14 days of purchase/delivery.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

How Exchanges Work

  • Select the new item you wish to exchange the original purchase for.
  • If the selected replacement item is priced higher, you will be required to pay the price difference.
  • If the selected replacement is priced lower, the remaining balance will be issued as store credit toward a future purchase. No refunds will be issued for the difference in value.

Returns/Exchanges That Will Not Be Accepted

  • Items sent back to us without first requesting a return.
  • Requests made beyond the 14-day window.
  • Items that are used, damaged, altered, or missing components.
  • Items not sent in their original packaging.
  • Items returned without prior approval from Siaram.

How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, please contact Client Services by email at customerservice@siaramjewelry.com or WhatsApp at +961 76 052 068.

Upon receipt of the returned item, Siaram Jewelry will inspect the piece to ensure it meets our return eligibility requirements. If the return is accepted, we will contact you and dispatch the selected replacement item, subject to availability. If the item does not meet our eligibility requirements, the exchange request may be declined and the item will be returned to you.

Approved returns are eligible for exchange only. Customers who return an eligible item without selecting a replacement will not receive a refund, store credit, or credit toward a future purchase. Refunds are only available for approved cases involving damaged or defective items — see Refunds below.

10. Gift Vouchers

How to Purchase a Gift Voucher

Looking for the perfect gift? Siaram offers personalised gift vouchers that allow your loved ones to choose a piece they truly adore.

To create your personalised gift voucher, please provide:

  • The voucher amount (e.g. $150, $250, or any value of your choice).
  • The full name of the recipient.
  • The full name of the purchaser (the person gifting the voucher).

Once payment has been received, a personalised Siaram gift voucher will be issued and sent to the purchaser digitally.

Gift Voucher Terms

  • Gift vouchers are issued for the exact value purchased.
  • Gift vouchers may be redeemed toward any Siaram Jewelry purchase.
  • If the selected item exceeds the value of the voucher, the recipient is responsible for paying the remaining balance.
  • If the selected item is less than the value of the voucher, any remaining balance will be issued as store credit toward a future purchase.
  • Gift vouchers are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Lost, stolen, or expired gift vouchers cannot be replaced.
  • Each gift voucher is issued with a unique voucher reference number for verification and redemption purposes.
  • Gift vouchers are personalised for the named recipient and are non-transferable. Only the individual named on the voucher may redeem it; it cannot be transferred, gifted, sold, or redeemed by any other individual. The recipient's ID will be checked before redemption.
  • Gift vouchers are valid for 6 months from the date of purchase.
